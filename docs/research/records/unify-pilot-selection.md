# Pilot Product Evaluation — Unify

Status: In progress

## Decision evidence

- Stakeholder selection date: 2026-07-30
- Selected pilot: Unify
- Stated rationale: Unify will be redesigned
- Redesign scope: The whole product
- Business context: Rancard is primarily B2B; Unify also serves B2C use cases
- Decision participants: Not provided

## Evaluation criteria

| Criterion                           | Current evidence                               |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Representative shared UI needs      | Not yet assessed                               |
| Engineering integration feasibility | Not yet assessed                               |
| Product-team capacity               | Redesign intent stated; capacity not confirmed |
| Accessibility learning value        | Not yet assessed                               |
| Delivery risk                       | Not yet assessed                               |
| Adoption measurement                | Not yet defined                                |

## Decision

Unify is the stakeholder-selected pilot product for the next stages.

The selection identifies the research focus. It does not approve token values,
component APIs, a delivery date, or a production release.

## Desired outcomes

- faster task completion
- good navigation
- faster engineering delivery
- developer access through excellent documentation, searchable examples, easy
  installation, and clear contribution guidance

These outcomes are qualitative. Baselines and measurable targets have not yet
been defined.

## Required follow-up

- document Unify’s purpose and representative redesign journeys
- map its B2B and B2C user groups in detail
- record its web and administration-portal architecture
- identify redesign decision participants
- translate the desired outcomes into baselines and measurable pilot targets
- document constraints when they become available

## Evidence limitations

No Unify interface, analytics, source-repository, accessibility-test, journey,
or adoption evidence has been provided. The stakeholder accepted these
limitations for Stage 1. “Whole product” identifies breadth but does not yet
define a bounded pilot release.
