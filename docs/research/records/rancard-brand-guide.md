# Current-State Foundation Audit — Rancard Brand Guide

Status: In progress

This record captures existing brand guidance. It does not define approved
design-system tokens, production CSS variables, or Figma variables.

## Record metadata

- Category: Brand identity, color, typography, logo, and voice
- Products observed: Portfolio-level Rancard brand
- Source: `Rancard Brand Guide.pdf`, supplied by the stakeholder
- Source metadata title: `rancard-world-color-Master-final-web`
- Source creation and modification date: 2011-05-24
- Audit date: 2026-07-30
- Extraction method: PDF text extraction and visual verification of relevant
  pages

## Observed typography guidance

- The recommended brand family is Helvetica Neue in Light, Regular, and Bold.
- The guide assigns Light to small print, Regular to main text, and Bold to
  headings.
- For web main copy, the guide advises Arial based on its availability on PCs.
- Banner advertising and graphics containing text retain the Helvetica family.
- The brand signature uses Hand of Sean.

These are historical brand-guide observations, not approved product typography
tokens.

## Observed color guidance

The guide labels black and red as the primary palette and lists five supporting
colors:

| Secondary      | `#FF9B00`    |

The values are transcribed current-state evidence. They are not approved token
names or values.

## Observed logo guidance

- The standard logo uses black and red on white.
- The source permits black or red as the other logo backgrounds.
- On black or red, the source uses a reversed white logo.
- The guide provides logo construction dimensions and examples, but no digital
  minimum-size or responsive-use rule was identified in the reviewed section.

## Observed voice and promise

- Voice attributes: informal, human, progressive, and savvy.
- Brand promise: “Connect to possibility.”
- The guide describes the promise as enabling content providers, mobile
  networks, and end users to connect and achieve possibilities.

## Accessibility implications

- The guide predates the current design-system accessibility baseline.
- Palette membership does not establish accessible contrast for text, icons,
  focus indicators, or interaction states.
- Font recommendations do not establish readable product type sizes, line
  heights, fallback behavior, or language coverage.
- Logo restrictions require validation for contemporary digital surfaces.

## Questions requiring confirmation

- The guide is historical guidance pending an update after the design-system
  work.
- The existing logo, red-and-black identity, “Connect to possibility” promise,
  and informal, human, progressive, and savvy voice remain current.
- Helvetica Neue should remain the provisional typography direction.
- Helvetica Neue licensing and delivery still require confirmation.
- The status and intended use of Hand of Sean still require confirmation.
- A web fallback strategy still requires confirmation.
- Are there current logo assets and digital usage specifications?

## Evidence limitations

- The guide is confirmed as historical guidance pending a future update.
- It describes brand communication more than interactive product UI.
- No current product interfaces were reviewed.
- No contrast, typography, localization, or platform accessibility validation
  has been performed.
- The provisional Helvetica Neue direction is not an approved typography token
  or final type scale.
