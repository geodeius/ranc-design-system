# Stage 1 Plan — Research and Audit

Status: Complete

## Objective

Build an evidence-backed view of the existing Rancard product ecosystem before
defining shared foundations or selecting pilot components.

## Assumptions

- Product evidence and team access have not yet been provided in this
  repository.
- Repository templates may be created without deciding the outcome of the
  research.
- Product names, owners, priorities, and technology stacks must be supplied or
  directly evidenced.
- Existing registry entries describe the design-system platform, not the
  current product ecosystem.

## Risks

- Incomplete product coverage could bias foundation and component priorities.
- Screenshots can expose personal, customer, or production data.
- Similar appearance does not prove shared behavior or implementation.
- Interview claims can conflict with source-code or interface evidence.
- Selecting a pilot before engineering and accessibility constraints are known
  could invalidate later work.

## Exit criteria

- [x] At least one pilot product is explicitly selected.
- [x] High-frequency shared UI needs are qualitatively identified.
- [x] Initial product constraints are documented.
- [x] Existing technical dependencies are understood at an accepted preliminary
      level.
- [x] Research limitations and uncovered evidence are documented.
- [x] No research placeholders are presented as approved specifications.

## Immediate inputs required

- list of in-scope products and environments
- safe access to representative interfaces or sanitized screenshots
- source repositories or dependency manifests where engineering audit is
  authorized
- product-team contacts and permission to record non-sensitive interview notes
- known accessibility reports or testing environments
- pilot-selection constraints and decision participants
