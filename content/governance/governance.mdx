**Draft**

This page summarizes the governance model already established by repository policy. It does not assign undocumented people or teams.

## Core-team responsibilities

The design-system core team owns architecture, standards, final approvals, releases, and governance.

## Contributor responsibilities

Contributors provide proposals, documentation, exploration, implementation, and testing within the repository rules.

## Product-team responsibilities

Product teams provide adoption feedback, usage validation, bug reports, and evidence for shared needs.

## Required review disciplines

Relevant changes require design, engineering, accessibility, content, and product review according to scope.

## Decision flow

Requests move through triage, research, proposal, review, implementation, testing, documentation, release, and adoption measurement.

## Architecture decisions

Major architectural changes require an ADR. Accepted ADRs and authoritative planning documents cannot be silently overridden.

## Ownership status

Concrete GitHub ownership identities remain unresolved. The repository uses the documented `design-systems` ownership label without inventing individuals.
